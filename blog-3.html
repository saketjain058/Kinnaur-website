A Complete Guide to Chitkul

Destinations Jul 10, 2024
Home / Blog / A Complete Guide to Chitkul

Chitkul is the last inhabited village on the old Hindustan-Tibet road. Here is everything you need to know before you go.

How to Reach

From Shimla, drive to Sangla via Karcham, then onward to Chitkul — roughly a full day's journey. The final stretch along the Baspa river is spectacular.

What to See

  • The wooden Mathi Devi temple
  • The last dhaba of India
  • Baspa riverside meadows
  • Traditional Kinnauri houses with slate roofs

Where to Stay

Chitkul has cozy homestays and a few guesthouses. Nights are cold even in summer, so pack warm layers.

Tips

  • Carry cash — limited connectivity and no ATMs
  • Acclimatize; the village sits above 3,400m
  • Respect local customs and the fragile environment
