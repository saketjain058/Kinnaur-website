Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your information.

Home / Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 January 2026

At Incredible Kinnaur Holidays, your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website or book a tour, and how we handle it.

Information We Collect

When you contact us or request a quote, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, travel dates and trip preferences. This is provided voluntarily through our enquiry and contact forms.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your enquiries and prepare personalized itineraries.
  • To confirm bookings and share travel-related updates.
  • To improve our services and website experience.
  • To send occasional offers — only if you have opted in.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your personal data. Information may be shared only with trusted local partners (hotels, drivers, guides) strictly to fulfil your booking.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Your Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by emailing us at incrediblekinnaurholidays@gmail.com.

Contact Us

For any questions about this Privacy Policy, reach us at incrediblekinnaurholidays@gmail.com or call +91 82192 09660.
