Chandratal: The Moon Lake of Spiti

Destinations Jun 22, 2024
Home / Blog / Chandratal: The Moon Lake of Spiti

Crescent-shaped and impossibly blue, Chandratal — the Moon Lake — is one of the most magical sights in the Himalayas.

The Legend

Local lore says the lake is where fairies descend at night. Its shifting colours through the day only add to the mystique.

Getting There

Chandratal lies near the Kunzum Pass, reachable from both Spiti and Manali sides when the passes are open (roughly June to October). A short trek from the parking area leads to the shore.

Camping & Photography

  • Camp at designated sites a few km from the lake
  • Sunrise and sunset offer the best light
  • Night skies here are extraordinary for astrophotography

Know Before You Go

High altitude (~4,300m) means thin air and cold nights. Acclimatize in Spiti first and travel with an experienced team.
